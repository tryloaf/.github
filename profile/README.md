loaf is a native Mac browser i’m designing and building with Swift and WebKit.

i’m [Owen Van Vooren](https://owen.uno), a designer and developer from Minnesota. i work on apps, websites, and interfaces. this is where i’m keeping loaf and the things around it.

tabs, profiles, and media sit in the sidebar. downloads, history, and favorites are in the tray. the wonderbar handles addresses and search. there’s also split view, a customizable start page, and experimental Chrome extension support.

browsing history stays on your Mac. passwords stay in Keychain. loaf doesn’t collect analytics or telemetry. optional online features contact their providers; the [privacy policy](https://tryloaf.app/privacy) explains what gets sent and when.

still in development, with no public build yet. loaf will be free to use, and the source will be available to review at release. the [proprietary license](https://tryloaf.app/license) allows private builds for review and testing. it isn’t open source.
