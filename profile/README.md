### a browser that feels like home.

loaf is a native Mac browser, built in Swift and powered by WebKit.

i’m Owen, an interface designer and developer from Minnesota. i care about apps that feel considered, behave predictably, and earn the space they take up. loaf is where that’s been going lately.

tabs, profiles, and media live in the sidebar. downloads, history, and favorites live in the tray. the wonderbar gets you where you’re going. there’s split view, a customizable start page, and experimental Chrome extension support, too.

browsing history stays on your Mac. passwords stay in Keychain. no analytics or telemetry from loaf. optional online features still contact their providers; the [privacy policy](https://tryloaf.app/privacy) covers the details.

still in development. the first public release is on its way. loaf will be free to use, with source available to inspect under a [proprietary license](https://tryloaf.app/license). it won’t be open source.
